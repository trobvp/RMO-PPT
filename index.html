Aim and need

Why this system is required

Aim

Build one integrated digital system to monitor and operate a remote lobby from the admin lobby in real time. It should keep operations safe, use manpower better, and make every step transparent and accountable.

In practice, the BTD and JND lobbies are operated from the BVC Lobby.

Existing system
  • Each lobby has 1 CTCC/CCCR and 3 TCC/CCR supervisors, one per shift.
  • Data entry operators / TNCs are also needed at each lobby.
  • Crew and Train Managers report to the lobby and Sign ON/OFF on CMS, with a built-in Breath Analyzer (BA) test and CCTV monitoring.
  • Crew read instructions and fill the registers by hand, in hard copy.
Key issues in the existing system
1

Casual sign-on and sign-off

CMS Sign-ON/OFF has become routine for lobby staff and crew. Manual sign-on, improper BA tests, or tests by an unauthorized person are possible. Nothing verifies that crew have read and understood safety instructions.

2

Bypassed checks

Crew can bypass critical checks: wrong or deliberate manual Sign ON/OFF, or a BA test not done properly or not done by the right person.

3

Manpower constraints

Small lobbies have few crew but still need staff. Overall, a lot of manpower is required and many posts are vacant. At traffic lobbies the Station Master cannot monitor properly while doing several tasks at once.

4

Paper registers

Multiple hard-copy registers are kept. Sample checks happen, but full data analysis is not possible.

5

Geographical dispersion

Many crew lobbies sit at distant operating locations across the division, so continuous physical oversight is hard. Centralized monitoring and operation is not possible.

6

Conventional methods in a digital era

Lobbies still run on conventional methods. Digital technology allows real-time supervision, transparent audit trails and streamlined control from the admin lobby.

Standard procedure

Crew Sign ON and Sign OFF

Both procedures follow the same five steps. The admin lobby gives final approval after live monitoring.

  1. Crew reportingCrew report for duty and make their entry in the registers digitally, on the mobile app.
  2. CommunicationCrew sit in front of the designated PC at the remote lobby and open an audio/video link to the Admin Lobby supervisor, DEO or RMO Sarthi (AI chatbot).
  3. Crew verificationIdentity checked by AI face recognition or by the supervisor, using name and Crew ID.
  4. Safety checkA few questions from drives, circulars and operational/technical topics (changed regularly as circulars change). Crew confirm receipt of safety equipment: walkie-talkie, detonators and FSD.
  5. CMS Sign ONAfter the procedure and live monitoring, the admin lobby gives final approval.

RMO monitoring screen during Sign ON

  1. Crew reportingCrew report after completing duty.
  2. CommunicationCrew sit in front of the designated PC at the remote lobby and open an audio/video link to the Admin Lobby supervisor, DEO or RMO Sarthi (AI chatbot).
  3. Crew verificationIdentity checked by AI face recognition or by the supervisor, using name and Crew ID.
  4. Detail confirmationTrain worked, section, and return of safety equipment. Any operational abnormality or incident is reported.
  5. CMS Sign OFFAfter the procedure and live monitoring, the admin lobby gives final approval. The entry goes into the registers digitally, on the mobile app.

What changes

Benefits of RMO implementation

Centralized supervision

One admin hub oversees geographically spread crew lobbies.

Manpower optimization

Fewer supervisors are needed at each remote lobby.

Transparency & control

Real-time visual monitoring of Sign ON/OFF and Breathalyzer compliance. Better accountability in crew management and record keeping.

Enhanced safety

Mandatory verification of identity, equipment collection (walkie-talkie, FSD, detonators), rest hours and circulars.

Digital audit trail

Operational records are available instantly for review and compliance analysis.

Benefits at traffic lobbies
A

Lower workload for the Station Master, who holds a safety-category post and needs to focus on train operations.

B

Better TRO statistics, as loco lobby staff become more familiar with CMS: fewer BOR, WOR, continuous night duty and PR-due cases.

C

Less delay in Sign ON/OFF, which sometimes waits on the Station Master's routine work.

When something fails

Contingency protocols and system failure management

⚠ CMS failure

Follow the prescribed manual Sign ON/OFF procedure. The Breathalyzer test is mandatory, under camera monitoring and video recording at the BVC Admin Lobby.

⚠ RMO application failure

Switch at once to the designated alternative communication channels and the manual verification steps in the SOP.

⚠ Complete system failure

The remote DEO alerts the CLI / CCCR at BVC immediately and follows the emergency manual operating instructions.

⚠ Network / link issues

Report the connectivity problem promptly through the dedicated RMO technical communication group for quick restoration.

Core principleKeep lobby operations fully continuous without compromising any prescribed safety requirement.

What comes next

Challenges and the way ahead

Challenges

Power disruptions

Unannounced power outages at remote locations.

Network connectivity

System effectiveness depends on reliable internet.

Technical site disruptions

Temporary app or website maintenance, or hardware failure.

Non-adherence

Some crew find it hard to adapt to new digital systems.

Way ahead for stabilization

1. Rapid fault resolution

Prompt action to fix power, network, CMS and application issues.

2. System adherence

Run RMO alongside the existing system at first. Give regular training and awareness for smooth adoption.

3. Inter-departmental coordination

Work closely with S&T and Operating branches.

Future scope and technology roadmap

AI chatbot for crew interaction

Explore an AI-powered chatbot to help crew interact and give operational assistance.

AI-based crew state monitoring

Explore real-time CCTV video analysis to spot crew alertness, fatigue and other safety-related conditions, for safer train operations.

These features are currently being implemented on a trial basis.

Digital supervision, disciplined procedures and reliable connectivity together strengthen safe and efficient railway operations.
